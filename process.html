Step-By-Step Journey

How we bring your dream media wall to life.

Whether you're building a sleek linear fireplace feature or full-wall acoustic panelling with shaker storage, here is what to expect.

01

Consultation & Instant Quote

Send us a photo of your wall, your rough room measurements, and any inspiration images. We'll assess your space and provide an instant, accurate quotation within minutes.

02

Bespoke Specification & Design

Once you're happy to move forward, we finalize every dimension around your exact TV model, soundbar, electric fire specifications, and preferred storage door finishes.

03

3-Day On-Site Build & Fit

Our dedicated team arrives on site. We build the heavy-duty timber stud frame, complete all electrical wiring and socket recesses, install alcove lighting, and apply smooth plastering.

04

Handover, Waste Removal & Guarantee

We perform a full cleanup, legally dispose of all construction materials and packaging, conduct a final walkthrough with you, and hand over your 5-year structural guarantee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our installations.

Do I need to hire an electrician or plasterer separately?

No! Canvas Media Walls handles the complete end-to-end supply and fit. Our team takes care of timber framing, electrical spur installation, TV bracket mounting, spotlights, and smooth plastering in one package.

How long does plaster take to dry before I can paint?

Typically 3 to 5 days depending on your room ventilation. We provide complete painting guidance or can recommend our professional decorating add-on service.

Can I change my TV size in the future?

Yes. We design TV recesses with generous ventilation and future-proofing in mind, or we can build flat-fronted floating walls with universal TV mounts for easy upgrades.

Do you supply the electric fireplaces?

We supply top-tier panoramic 3-sided electric fires with realistic flame effects and climate controls, or we can seamlessly build your wall around a fireplace you have already purchased.
